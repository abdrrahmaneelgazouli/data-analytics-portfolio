## 🛒 Project 1: Supermarket Sales Report - Q1 2019

**Tools:** Power BI | DAX | Data Visualization

**Dashboard:**
![Sales Report Q1 2019](sales-report-q1-2019-fixed.png)

**Overview:**
Analyzed 1000+ supermarket transactions worth $169.12K in Q1 2019 across 3 branches (A, B, C) and 3 cities.

**KPIs:**
- Total Sales: $169.12K
- Total COGS: $161.06K
- Gross Income: $8.05K

**Key Insights:**
- Top City: Naypyitaw - $60.99K (36.07% of total sales)
- Top Branch: Branch C - $60.99K
- Top Product Line: Sports and Travel - $32.09K
- Gender: Male customers generated slightly more revenue than Female
- Monthly Trend: Sales dropped in Feb (48K) then peaked in March (62K) - V-shape recovery
- Product Performance: 6 product lines almost equally distributed, lowest is Food and Beverages (26.33K)

**Visuals Used:** Bar Chart, Donut Chart, Pie Chart, Line Chart, Treemap, Card KPIs

**Business Recommendation:**
Focus marketing on Naypyitaw city and Sports category, investigate February drop.

## ☕ Project 3: Costa Cafe Sales Dashboard

**Tools:** Excel | Pivot Charts | Slicers | Dashboard Design

**Dashboard:**
![Costa Cafe Dashboard](costa-cafe-enhanced.png)

**Overview:**
Analyzed 1,313 coffee orders worth $4,267.58 across 8 menu items to understand sales performance, customer preferences, and operational peaks.

**KPIs:**
- Total Sales: $4,267.58
- Total Orders: 1,313
- Total Items: 8

**Key Insights:**
- Best Seller: Americano with Milk (319 orders) - Top product, followed by Latte (282)
- Worst Seller: Cocoa (44) and Espresso (59) - Need promotion or removal
- Peak Month: May with 267 orders, Lowest: August with 180 orders
- Best Day: Tuesday (206 orders), Worst: Sunday (172)
- Peak Shift: Evening (469 orders) > Afternoon (435) > Morning (409)
- Payment: 93.2% of customers use one payment method (likely Card/Cash), only 6.8% use alternative - System is heavily dependent on one method
- Balanced week: Orders are almost evenly distributed (172-206), no dead day

**Visuals Used:** Bar Chart, Line Chart (Trend), Treemap (by Day), Pie Chart (Payment), Horizontal Bar (Shifts), Slicer (Day filter)

**Business Recommendations:**
1. Push Americano with Milk as signature drink - high demand
2. Staff more people in Evening shift, less in Morning
3. Investigate August sales drop (180 orders) - seasonality?
4. Promote Cocoa and Espresso with combos


## 📊 Project 4: Data Professional Survey Breakdown - 630 Professionals

**Tools:** Power BI | Survey Analysis | DAX | Data Storytelling

**Dashboard:**
![Data Professional Survey](data-professional-survey.png)

**Overview:**
Analyzed survey of 630 data professionals worldwide (Average Age: 29.87) to understand job market, salary, skills, and job satisfaction.

**KPIs:**
- Survey Takers: 630
- Average Age: 29.87 years
- Work/Life Happiness: 5.74 / 10
- Salary Happiness: 4.27 / 10

**Key Insights:**
- **Geography:** Majority from United States, followed by Other countries, India is 3rd
- **Salary by Role:** Data Scientist has highest average salary, followed by Data Engineer and Data Architect. Student/Looking has lowest
- **Top Language:** Python is dominant favorite (400+ votes) across all job titles, R is second, Java is least favorite
- **Difficulty to Break In:** 42.7% say Neither easy nor difficult, 24.76% say Difficult, 21.27% say Easy, only 6.98% say Very Easy
- **Happiness:** Work/Life balance (5.74) higher than Salary happiness (4.27) - Professionals are more satisfied with work-life than pay
- **Market:** Shows data field is competitive but achievable, Python is must-learn skill

**Visuals Used:** Treemap, Bar Chart, Stacked Bar, Donut Chart, Gauge Charts, KPI Cards

**Business Insight:**
For aspiring Data Analysts: Learn Python first, target Data Scientist path for highest salary growth, expect moderate difficulty entering field, but job satisfaction is decent.
