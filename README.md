## 🛒 Project 1: Supermarket Sales Report - Q1 2019

**Tools:** Power BI | DAX | Data Visualization

**Dashboard:**
![Sales Report Q1 2019](sales-report-q1-2019-fixed.png)

**Overview:**
Analyzed 1000+ supermarket transactions worth $169.12K in Q1 2019 across 3 branches (A, B, C) and 3 cities.

**KPIs:**
- Total Sales: $169.12K
- Total COGS: $161.06K
- Gross Income: $8.05K

**Key Insights:**
- Top City: Naypyitaw - $60.99K (36.07% of total sales)
- Top Branch: Branch C - $60.99K
- Top Product Line: Sports and Travel - $32.09K
- Gender: Male customers generated slightly more revenue than Female
- Monthly Trend: Sales dropped in Feb (48K) then peaked in March (62K) - V-shape recovery
- Product Performance: 6 product lines almost equally distributed, lowest is Food and Beverages (26.33K)

**Visuals Used:** Bar Chart, Donut Chart, Pie Chart, Line Chart, Treemap, Card KPIs

**Business Recommendation:**
Focus marketing on Naypyitaw city and Sports category, investigate February drop.







## 🚲 Project 2: Bike Sales Dashboard - Customer Behavior Analysis

**Tools:** Excel | Pivot Charts | Slicers | Data Analysis

**Dashboard:**
![Bike Dashboard](bike2_enhanced.png)

**What I Analyzed:**
Customer dataset to understand who buys bikes based on Income, Gender, Age, and Commute Distance.

**Key Insights:**
- Avg Income of Bike Buyers is higher than Non-Buyers (Female: 59k vs 54k, Male: 61k vs 59k)
- Middle Age is the top buyer segment with ~200 purchases
- Customers with 0-1 Miles commute distance have the highest purchase rate (100+)
- Purchase rate drops significantly when commute > 10 Miles
- Married customers filtered - shows core target audience

**Filters Used:** Marital Status, Gender, Age Brackets, Commute Distance
